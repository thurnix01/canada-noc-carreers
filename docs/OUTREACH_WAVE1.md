Keep it short. One courtesy note, three asks, no funding language.

**Subject:** A public search that sends people to {Community}’s RCIP portal

```
Hello {Name},

I’m Terence Absolon with Absolon Designs. I built a free search so people can see which of the 14 RCIP communities list their occupation or a designated employer — then continue on that community’s official site:

https://noccareers.ca

It isn’t a competitor to your portal. We don’t take applications or give immigration advice. A designated employer is not treated as “hiring.” Where your public lists allow it, we show last-verified dates and Job Bank links, and we always send the next step back to you.

Happy to correct how we describe your process. A one-pager is available if useful.

Thank you,

```

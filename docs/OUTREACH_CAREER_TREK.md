# Soft outreach — WorkBC Career Trek citation

**Goal:** Courtesy notice that noccareers.ca links to public Career Trek videos for matching B.C. RCIP NOCs. Ask preferred attribution / guidelines. **Not** a funding or YouTube co-branding ask.

**When to send:** After the Career Trek button is live on [noccareers.ca](https://noccareers.ca) (filter to a B.C. community, open an Eligible NOC that has a video — e.g. NOC 42202).

**From:** Your name · Absolon Designs · `info@noccareers.ca`

**Suggested contact paths (try in order):**

1. WorkBC Support / contact form on [workbc.ca](https://www.workbc.ca/) — note “Career Trek resource citation”
2. Any Career Trek or web-content contact listed on WorkBC pages
3. Only use `CEPTEAM@gov.bc.ca` if they redirect you there (that inbox is mainly **funding**, not citations)

## Email copy

**Subject:** Citing WorkBC Career Trek videos next to BC RCIP occupations on noccareers.ca

```
Hello Career Trek / WorkBC team,

I’m {Your name} with Absolon Designs. I run an unofficial public search aid for Rural Community Immigration Pilot (RCIP) priority occupations and designated employers:

https://noccareers.ca

The tool does not give immigration advice or host applications. It helps people find eligible NOCs and designated employers, then sends them to the official community portal and Job Bank.

For B.C. RCIP communities, we now link matching WorkBC Career Trek videos on eligible NOC cards (where a video exists), with clear credit to WorkBC / Career Trek. Example:

• Site: https://noccareers.ca (filter to a B.C. community → Eligible NOCs)
• Direct Career Trek page we cite for NOC 42202:
  https://www.workbc.ca/plan-career/career-trek-videos/42202/183

I’m writing to ask:

1) Whether you’re comfortable with this attribution approach,
2) Whether you prefer we link to Career Trek / WorkBC pages only (current approach) versus any other citation format, and
3) If there’s a preferred contact for resource-citation questions going forward.

Happy to adjust wording or remove links if needed. Thank you for the Career Trek library — it’s useful career context alongside RCIP eligibility, not a substitute for your tools or IRCC.

{Your name}
Absolon Designs
info@noccareers.ca · https://noccareers.ca
```

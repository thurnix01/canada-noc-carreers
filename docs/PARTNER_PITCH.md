## The problem

Overseas candidates and new immigrants must open multiple community portals, PDF employer lists, and priority-NOC pages just to answer: *“Is my NOC eligible here, and where do I apply for a real job?”*  
Canada.ca only links out. Job Bank has no RCIP filter. Consultant blogs go stale.

## What we are building

A **free, unofficial curated aggregator** (GitHub Pages) for candidates that:

1. Lets them search by **NOC**, community, and sector  
2. Surfaces **priority NOCs**, **designated employers**, and **public job links** (when available)  
3. **Always links back** to your official pages / Job Bank / Indeed — we do not host applications or give immigration advice  
4. Shows clear labels: *Eligible NOC* · *Designated employer* · *Open posting*  
5. Repeats your guidance: **do not mass-email designated employers** unless a role is publicly advertised  

Pilot focus: **West Kootenay · North Okanagan–Shuswap · Peace Liard**, then expand to all 14 RCIP communities.

## What we are *not*

- Not IRCC-affiliated and not a replacement for your portal  
- Not a job-matching or resume dump service  
- Not encouraging unsolicited outreach to designated employers  
- Not scraping private portals or ignoring robots.txt (light, respectful public-page refresh)

## Why partner with you

| For your office | For candidates |
|---|---|
| Extra discovery traffic to your official URLs | One place to filter by NOC across BC RCIP sites |
| Fewer “where do I look?” emails | Clear next step: apply to posted jobs / follow your guides |
| Optional co-branding / “official sources” callout | Updated when your public lists change |

We will share **outbound click metrics** (via Microsoft Clarity + simple link tracking) so you can see referrals to your site.

## Ask (lightweight)

1. **Permission to index** your publicly published priority NOCs, designated employer lists, and job-guidance pages, with attribution and “last updated” dates.  
2. A **contact** for list-change notices (or we poll your public pages on a slow schedule).  
3. Optional: a one-line acknowledgement on your candidate page (“Unofficial search aid: [URL]”) once the pilot is live.

No cost to you for the pilot. If useful later, we can discuss sponsoring maintenance or a joint settlement/ED funding proposal with an eligible organization as lead applicant.
